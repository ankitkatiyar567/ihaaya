⭐ How Ihaaya works

Shine a little brighter every day.

StartEvery dayStarsGame, story, report Parent menuSettingsYour data

🚀 Start (2 minutes)

  1. Add it to the phone as an app: iPhone Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. Android Chrome → ⋮ → Install app.
  2. Choose a 4‑digit parent PIN that only grown‑ups know.
  3. Type your child's name and pick a picture. That's it.

🌙 Every day

  • 👆 Tap a picture to hear its question read aloud.
  • Your child answers: 1–5 ⭐ if it went well, 1–5 ❌ if not. Tap the same one again to clear it.
  • Grown‑ups tap + on 😢 each time they cry. Taking a cry back needs the PIN.
  • Add a 💌 lovely moment if you like; it shows in the weekly report.

⭐ How stars count

  • Stars add, crosses take away, each cry takes 2 stars away. A day never goes below 0.
  • Score = stars ÷ the most possible (5 per rated picture). Goal 60% ≈ 3 stars a picture.
  • Stars also grow levels: 🌱 → 🌷 → 🐣 → 🦋 → … → 👑.

🎁 Game, story and report

  • 🎮 Daily game: opens when every picture is rated and the day reaches the goal. 3 minutes a day, one of six games.
  • 🌙 Bedtime story: every night, no stars needed. 7–10 minutes, never one already heard. Theme pictures or 🎲 pick another. Needs internet.
  • 🎁 Weekly report: on the report day (Friday): running stars when the week reaches the goal, gentle tips when not.

👪 Parent menu

Tap the child's picture at the top right, then the PIN.

  • Switch child · ＋ Add a child · ✏️ edit name and picture · 🗑️ remove a child
  • ⚙️ Settings · 🔤 words under pictures · 💾 backup · 🔢 change or turn off the PIN · 🔒 lock

⚙️ Settings

For the child shown now, except "Sounds and words" (for this phone).

Name, picture, report day, goal, reward

The weekly report day (the week ends on it), the goal % (default 60%), and a reward shown when the goal is reached.

🎮 Daily game

On or off, and 2, 3 or 5 minutes a day.

🌙 Bedtime story

On or off. "Forget the stories heard" lets old stories come again.

🖼️ Pictures (tasks)

Tick the tasks to use, change the emoji, add a 📷 photo (her own toothbrush, her bed), edit the question that is read aloud, or add your own task.

🔊 Sounds and words

Happy sounds, reading questions aloud, the voice language (English, Hindi, Finnish…), and words under the pictures.

💾 Backup and reset

Download a backup now and then; restore it on another phone to move the stars. "Clear stars" starts a child's chart fresh; "Remove" deletes the child.

🔒 Your data and the PIN

  • Stars, notes and photos stay on this phone only; nothing is sent anywhere. Each phone keeps its own, so use one phone or move them with a backup.
  • The PIN keeps little fingers out of the parent menu. Forgot it? Tap Forgot the PIN? and type RESET: the PIN turns off and the stars stay.
  • Stories are free recordings from LibriVox.org, played from the Internet Archive.
